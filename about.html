WHO WE ARE

Built in Anyiin for practical learning.

Ashi Polytechnic is a private polytechnic located in Anyiin, Logo Local Government Area of Benue State. National technical-education records list the institution as established in 2018.

The institution's public website describes it as an affiliate of Benue State University. In 2022, The Nation reported an MoU between Benue State University and Ashi Polytechnic to run degree programmes.

Location

Ashi Polytechnic Road, Anyiin Town, Logo LGA, Benue State.

Institution type

Private Polytechnic. Public NBTE records list 2018 as establishment year.

Academic direction

ND and HND pathways across computing, engineering, business and related fields.
