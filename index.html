ABOUT ASHI POLYTECHNIC

Where practical education meets opportunity.

Ashi Polytechnic, Anyiin is a private polytechnic located in Anyiin, Logo Local Government Area of Benue State. The institution is listed in the Federal Ministry of Education's directory of approved private polytechnics, while NBTE directories identify the institution as established in 2018.

This presentation site is designed as the public layer of a future Digital Campus: a single ecosystem for information, admissions, students, staff and institutional administration.
